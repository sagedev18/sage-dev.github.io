Six things, and nothing else

Every screen exists because a student needs it, not because a feature list looked impressive.

01

Planner

Subjects, tasks and due dates. Sort by what is closest, tick things off, and the list reorders itself.

02

Focus timer

A Pomodoro that logs itself. Sessions build up into a chart so you can see the weeks you actually worked.

03

Notes

One note per topic, tagged to a subject. Written plainly, so they are useful at 2am the night before.

04

Flashcards

Decks with a know or do not know answer. Cards you keep missing resurface on their own.

05

Exams and syllabus

A live countdown per exam, with a topic checklist so nothing in the syllabus gets quietly skipped.

06

Statistics

Lifetime totals that never reset. The honest answer to whether the effort is landing.

What it is made of

Node.js and Express on the server, SQLite for storage, and plain JavaScript in the browser. No build step, no framework, nothing that has to be recompiled to fix a bug.

Your notes are nobody else's business

Every account is separate. One student cannot see another student's subjects, notes or exam dates, and that is enforced on the server rather than hidden in the interface.

  • Accounts are isolated at the database level
  • Admins see accounts, never a student's notes
  • Entering the admin console needs a single-use code
  • Passwords are hashed, never stored as text
  • Export or delete everything whenever you like
